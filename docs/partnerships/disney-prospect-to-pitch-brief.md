## 2. Prospect research

**AI posture:** Disney has stood up an internal AI task force and, on the Q3 2026 earnings call, framed AI as a "velocity accelerator" for human-centered storytelling — applications across trip planning, streaming personalization, staffing forecasts, and attraction design stress-testing. Josh D'Amaro: AI "enhancing a creative process." Language to mirror: *storytelling, creativity enhanced (not replaced) by technology, human-centered*.

**Philanthropy:** **Disney Future Storytellers** is the signature initiative ("empowering the next generation of diverse creators and innovators"), with $1M+ STEM/arts grant rounds to orgs including Girls Who Code, Hidden Genius Project, and Chicas en Tecnología. **VoluntEARS** is the employee-service engine (14M+ cumulative hours) — the natural home for a 5-volunteers-per-session ask.

**The wedge:** Disney already funds girls-in-tech orgs, but Girls Who Code teaches coding; Technovation is the only one that puts LA students and Disney employees at the same table building solutions to LA problems, with a season of visible student work traceable back to one Friday in their building — Future Storytellers made literal and local, three miles from the Burbank lot, at near-zero cost (a room + volunteers).

**Smart questions for the call:**
- How is the AI task force thinking about employee AI fluency beyond the product org?
- Would Future Storytellers want the students' season submissions showcased back to Disney in spring?
- Is VoluntEARS the right channel to source the ~10 volunteers, or does a studio CSR team adopt this?
